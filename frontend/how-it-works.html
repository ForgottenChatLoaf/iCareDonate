How iCareDonate Works

A step-by-step transparent process built to protect donors, empower requesters, and eliminate waste.

Who We Serve

iCareDonate serves low-income families, students in need of academic tools, disaster-affected communities, and community day care centers.

By cutting out middlemen and shipping costs, we guarantee 100% of the donated value reaches recipients directly.

The Three-Step Journey

From posting to safe community handovers.

1

Step 1: Listing or Requesting Items

For Donors: Fill out a quick form describing your available physical items (quantity, condition, photos, and preferred pick-up barangay).

For Requesters: Select needed items from our categories or browse active listings. Attach verifiable proof (such as student ID, barangay indigency certificate, or situational photos).

2

Step 2: Admin Inspection & Verification

Our administrative team checks every item submission for safety, cleanliness, and completeness. Requesters are verified against legitimate local community needs to eliminate duplicates and abuse.

Average verification turnaround is between 4 and 24 hours.

3

Step 3: Direct Community Pick-up & Handover

Once matched, the requester receives a pickup confirmation token and instructions. Both parties meet at designated public venues (barangay multi-purpose hall, community center, or school entrance) for safe handover.

Status updates in real-time, closing the loop and recording community impact.
