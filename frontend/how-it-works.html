How it works

Everything you need to know about posting items, requesting aid, and safe handovers.

Community Families

Who we serve

iCareDonate serves low-income households, disaster-affected communities, students requiring educational resources, and local charitable initiatives that require essential material aid.

Our platform connects requesters directly with community members offering surplus items, eliminating intermediaries and ensuring dignified support.

What we do

We manage the end-to-end verification, matching, and monitoring of physical item donations and community requests.

Every posted item and request is reviewed by administrators before going live, guaranteeing transparency, fair quantity allocation, and reliable pickup coordination.

Every Donation Counts

The 5-Step Community Workflow

A transparent, verified process connecting donors with people who need assistance.

1

Post Item or Need

Donors list usable items with photos and condition. Community members submit assistance requests detailing what they need.

2

Coordinator Review

Submissions are reviewed by community administrators to verify appropriate condition, item categorization, and request context.

3

Match & Connect

Verified requests are matched with available donations. Both members are notified with claim codes and coordination instructions.

4

Safe Local Handover

Members coordinate safe, dignified handovers through designated community partner centers or agreed local drop-off points.

5

Verified Completion

Handover completion is acknowledged on the platform, ensuring community transparency, safety, and mutual accountability.

Explore Our Guides

Safety & Community Standards

iCareDonate prohibits cash solicitations. All item listings and requests are 100% free of charge. Physical handovers are conducted at designated community centers or verified pickup venues.
