Careers & Volunteer Opportunities

Help build compassionate, resilient neighborhoods by joining our volunteer team.

Why Volunteer With iCareDonate?

iCareDonate is powered by neighbors, youth volunteers, and community leaders who donate their time to ensure supplies reach real hands safely.

Whether you can spare 3 hours on weekends or desire an active leadership role in your barangay, your involvement makes a tangible difference.
