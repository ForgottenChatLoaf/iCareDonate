Step-by-Step Instructions

Platform Guides & Tutorials

Clear, practical walkthroughs for every step of your giving and receiving journey on iCareDonate.

1

How to Post an Item Donation

Donating surplus essentials helps low-income students, families, and community shelters across Metro Manila and surrounding provinces.

  1. Log in to your account and click the prominent Post Donation button in your sidebar navigation.
  2. Enter a descriptive title (e.g. "5 Elementary School Backpacks with Notebooks").
  3. Choose an accurate category such as School Supplies, Food & Groceries, or Clothing & Shoes.
  4. Specify the total quantity available, unit of measurement, item condition (New, Like New, or Good), and expiration date if perishable.
  5. Upload clear, well-lit photos showing the actual items and any packaging labels.
  6. Specify your preferred handover arrangement: Public Pickup at a barangay hall or designated drop-off hub, or direct delivery.
  7. Review and submit. Your listing will show as Pending Verification while community moderators inspect it.
Tip: You will receive an automated notification once your listing is approved and visible in the public catalog.
2

Browsing and Searching Available Resources

Only verified, active listings are displayed in the resource directory.

  1. From your user navigation, select Browse Resources.
  2. Use the search bar to query specific keywords, item types, or donor cities.
  3. Filter by Category (e.g. Medical & First Aid, Educational Books) or Condition to find exactly what you need.
  4. Inspect each resource card to check the available quantity remaining, condition, and location.
  5. Click on any card to view detailed specifications, pickup guidelines, and the Request This Resource option.
3

Submitting an Assistance Request

Requests must be justified with transparent information to ensure fair allocation to verified beneficiaries.

  1. On an available resource listing, click Request This Item (or open My Requests → Request New Items).
  2. Specify the exact quantity you require. Never request more than your immediate family or student need.
  3. Provide a genuine justification explaining who the beneficiary is and why assistance is needed.
  4. Select the urgency level: Low, Moderate, or High / Urgent.
  5. Attach required proof of need (see guide below).
  6. Submit the application. You will receive an official Request Reference ID (e.g., #REQ-201).
4

Uploading Verification Documents (Proof of Need)

To prevent scalping and ensure genuine community impact, assistance requests require standard Philippine supporting documents:

  • Barangay Certificate of Indigency — issued by your local barangay office.
  • Solo Parent ID — issued by your City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office.
  • Public School Enrollment Certificate / Valid Student ID — for educational materials, tablets, or uniforms.
  • Disability / Senior Citizen ID — for medical supplies, mobility aids, or specialized food packs.

All uploaded files are strictly confidential, access-controlled, and only reviewed by authorized coordinators. Never share sensitive personal documents via public comments.

5

Tracking Request Lifecycle & Coordinator Reviews

Your request moves through an organized verification and matching lifecycle:

1. Submitted
Application recorded.
2. Under Review
Proof verified by coordinator.
3. Matched & Scheduled
Allocation confirmed.
4. Claimed & Completed
Successful handover.

If coordinators require clearer photos or an additional document, your request will be marked Needs More Information with actionable feedback and a secure resubmit button.

6

Claim Codes, Handover Protocols, and Safe Pickup

Once a request is matched to an available donation, a formal Transaction & Claim record is generated:

  1. Navigate to Transactions & Claims in your user menu.
  2. Open the active transaction card to view the designated meeting venue (e.g. Barangay Central Multi-Purpose Hall) and pickup deadline.
  3. Locate your unique 6-digit Secure Claim Code (e.g., ICD-849201).
  4. Never post your claim code publicly. Only present it in person to the donor or community coordinator upon physical handover.
  5. Both parties confirm the handover in the app to mark the transaction complete and update community impact statistics.
7

Managing Profile, Privacy, and Account Security

Keep your information up to date through the comprehensive Profile & Settings center:

  • Personal Information: Update your full name, mobile contact, and general barangay/city.
  • Account Verification: Inspect your verified donor or requester status, view previous review notes, and check interview schedules.
  • Security: Change your password securely with real server-side validation. Review active login sessions and terminate unfamiliar devices.
  • Privacy & Location: Control whether your contact number is visible to matched community partners or strictly protected.
  • Notification Preferences: Toggle in-app alerts for match events, pickup reminders, and support ticket replies.

Our community support team is available to assist you with proof submissions, resource listings, and pickup scheduling.
