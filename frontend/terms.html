Legal & Guidelines

Terms of Service

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Community Agreement

By accessing or using iCareDonate, you agree to comply with our community standard of dignified, non-monetary assistance. All participants must act with honesty, respect, and goodwill toward fellow community members.

2. Strictly Non-Monetary Platform

iCareDonate is exclusively designed for the free sharing of physical resources. It is strictly prohibited to demand, offer, or negotiate monetary payment, handling fees, shipping charges, or any form of compensation for donated items.

3. Item Quality & Safety Standards

Donors warrant that all items listed are safe, hygienic, and in functional condition. Broken electronics, hazardous materials, weapons, expired consumable goods, or unwashed garments are strictly prohibited and will be rejected during verification.

4. Verification & Account Suspension

iCareDonate reserves the right to inspect listings, request identity verification documents, and reject submissions that do not meet community standards. Users found attempting to resell donated goods will face immediate account termination and reporting to local authorities.

5. Local Pick-up & Safety

Item handovers must occur at designated public locations (such as barangay halls, community centers, or school administration offices). Users participate in item pick-ups at their own discretion and are advised to follow all public safety protocols.

6. Contact & Questions

For inquiries or clarifications regarding these terms, please contact our legal desk via our contact form or by writing to legal@icaredonate.org.
