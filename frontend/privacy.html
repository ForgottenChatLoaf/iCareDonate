Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

iCareDonate respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting personal information collected through our website in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to facilitate item donations and claims, including:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, and mobile phone number.
  • Item details: Descriptions, photos, condition notes, and designated pick-up barangay.
  • Verification documents: Student IDs, certificates of indigency, or situational photos submitted by requesters.

3. Confidential Handling of Proof Documents

All proof documents (such as student cards, certificates, and ID photos) are treated as strictly confidential. They are never published on the public website and are accessible solely by certified platform administrators for the singular purpose of verifying need.

4. No Commercial Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user contact details or records to third parties, commercial advertisers, or outside marketers.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You retain the right to review, update, or request the permanent deletion of your account and submitted files once handovers are concluded. To request record deletion, contact our Data Privacy Desk.

6. Privacy Contact

If you have questions about your privacy rights or how your files are managed, email our Data Protection Officer at privacy@icaredonate.org.
