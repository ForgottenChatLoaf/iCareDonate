Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about platform policies and workflows.

iCareDonate connects community members with surplus usable items (books, school supplies, clothes, groceries) to neighbors and families who need them. Donors post available items, requesters submit assistance requests with eligibility verification, and coordinators facilitate safe handovers once matched.
No. iCareDonate is strictly a free community item-sharing platform. Buying, selling, or asking for monetary payments or processing fees is strictly prohibited. If anyone asks you for money in exchange for a donation, report it immediately to our support team.
We accept official Barangay Certificates of Indigency, valid Student IDs or Enrollment Assessment Forms (for educational materials), DSWD 4Ps beneficiary identification, or Senior Citizen / PWD identification cards. All submitted documents are kept strictly confidential.
For community safety, all pick-ups should be conducted in well-lit, public venues such as Barangay Halls, school administrative offices, community multi-purpose buildings, or designated public transport terminals. Never arrange meetings in secluded private locations.
To ensure fairness across all community members and give coordinators adequate time to review document authenticity, members may have one pending donation listing or one pending resource request undergoing review at any given time. Once reviewed, you may submit your next listing or request.
