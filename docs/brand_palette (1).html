Brand guide: logo, color palette, and typography.

Logo

A heart held by two hands shaking. The heart is the care, the handshake is giving and receiving. One hand is cream and the other is amber, standing for the donor and the requester meeting in the middle.

iCareDonate
Primary lockup, on cream or white
iCareDonate
Reversed lockup, on deep navy
Icon only: favicon, app icon, social avatar

How to use it

  • The wordmark is Poppins Bold with only "Care" in amber #F2B84B.
  • Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the heart's top lobe.
  • Smallest sizes: icon 24 px wide, full lockup 120 px wide.

Please don't

  • Recolor the heart or hands, and never use green.
  • Stretch, rotate, or add shadows to the logo.
  • Change the amber in "Care" or swap it for another color.
  • Place the logo on a busy photo without a solid backing.

Color palette

Cobalt blue brings trust, coral draws the eye to action, and warm cream keeps the pages easy on the eyes. There is no green anywhere in the system.
