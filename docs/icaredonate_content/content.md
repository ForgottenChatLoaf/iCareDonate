## ABOUT US

iCareDonate is a donation platform developed by Ambagan Inc. to make giving and receiving support more accessible. Built around the idea that technology can help people help each other, the platform connects donors with people in need through item-based donations.

**Our Mission**

To make giving and receiving support safer, simpler, and more accessible by connecting donors with people in need through a trusted item-based donation platform. We strive to make it easier for people to share useful items while providing those in need with a reliable way to receive support.

**Our Vision**

To build a trusted and supportive community where technology helps people help each other. We envision a future where giving and receiving support is accessible to everyone, making it easier for people to share what they have and make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

**Who We Serve**

iCareDonate serves people who want to give useful items and individuals or communities who need support. Whether you have something to share or are looking for assistance, iCareDonate helps connect people through item-based donations.

![Who We Serve](images/04_who-we-serve.jpg)

**What We Do**

iCareDonate connects donors with people in need through item-based donations. We provide a simple and accessible platform for sharing useful items while focusing on a safe, trusted, and genuine donation experience.
