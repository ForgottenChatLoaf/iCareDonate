[How to Post an Item Donation]

[Overview placeholder: Donating surplus essentials helps low-income students, families, and community shelters across Metro Manila and surrounding provinces.]

  1. [Log in to your account and click the prominent Post Donation button in your sidebar navigation.]
  2. [Enter a descriptive title, e.g. "5 Elementary School Backpacks with Notebooks".]
  3. [Choose an accurate category such as School Supplies, Food & Groceries, or Clothing & Shoes.]
  4. [Specify total quantity available, unit of measurement, item condition, and expiration date if perishable.]
  5. [Upload clear, well-lit photos showing actual items and any packaging labels.]
  6. [Specify preferred handover arrangement: Public Pickup at community hub or direct delivery.]
  7. [Review and submit. Listing enters Pending Verification while moderators inspect it.]
[Tip]: [You will receive an automated notification once your listing is approved and visible in the public catalog.]
2

[Browsing and Searching Available Resources]

[Overview placeholder: Only verified, active listings are displayed in the resource directory.]

  1. [From user navigation, select Browse Resources.]
  2. [Use the search bar to query specific keywords, item types, or donor cities.]
  3. [Filter by Category or Condition to find exactly what you need.]
  4. [Inspect each resource card to check quantity remaining, condition, and location.]
  5. [Click on any card to view detailed specifications and initiate request.]
3

[Submitting an Assistance Request]

[Overview placeholder: Requests must be justified with transparent information to ensure fair allocation to verified beneficiaries.]

  1. [On an available resource listing, click Request This Item.]
  2. [Specify the exact quantity you require. Never request more than immediate student/family need.]
  3. [Provide a genuine justification explaining who the beneficiary is and why assistance is needed.]
  4. [Select urgency level: Low, Moderate, or High / Urgent.]
  5. [Attach required proof of need document.]
  6. [Submit application to receive official Request Reference ID (e.g. #REQ-201).]
4

[Uploading Verification Documents (Proof of Need)]

[Overview placeholder: To prevent scalping and ensure genuine community impact, assistance requests require standard Philippine supporting documents:]

  • [Barangay Certificate of Indigency] — [Issued by local barangay office.]
  • [Solo Parent ID] — [Issued by City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office.]
  • [Public School Student ID / Enrollment Form] — [For educational items, tablets, uniforms.]
  • [Disability (PWD) / Senior Citizen ID] — [For specialized food packs or medical aids.]

[All uploaded files are access-controlled and reviewed strictly by authorized coordinators.]

[Claim Codes, Handover Protocols, and Safe Pickup]

[Overview placeholder: Once a request is matched to an available donation, a formal Transaction & Claim pass record is generated:]

  1. [Navigate to Claims & Handovers in your user navigation.]
  2. [Open active transaction to view designated meeting venue and pickup deadline.]
  3. [Locate unique 6-digit Secure Claim Code, e.g. ICD-849201.]
  4. [Never share claim code online. Only present in person to hub coordinator during physical handover.]
  5. [Both parties confirm handover in app to mark transaction complete.]

[Managing Profile, Privacy, and Account Security]

[Overview placeholder: Keep your information up to date through the comprehensive Profile & Settings center:]

  • [Personal Information]: [Update full name, phone contact, and barangay location.]
  • [Account Verification]: [Inspect verified status and view interview schedules.]
  • [Security]: [Change password securely and review active login sessions.]
  • [Notification Preferences]: [Toggle alerts for match events and pickup reminders.]
