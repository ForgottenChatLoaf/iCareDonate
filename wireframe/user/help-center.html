[Q: How long does moderator approval take for posted donations?]

[A: Most listings are reviewed by community coordinators within 12 to 24 hours to ensure safety and accurate categorisation.]

[Q: What should I do if my proof document is marked "Needs More Info"?]

[A: Open My Requests → Request Details, review the coordinator notes, and use the re-upload button to submit a clearer photo.]

[A: Zero fees. iCareDonate is strictly free for all community donors and verified beneficiaries.]
