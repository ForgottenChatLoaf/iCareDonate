About us

Our mission, vision, and the community principles guiding iCareDonate.

Mission

To empower communities through direct, transparent, and verified sharing of essential material resources, bridging the gap between donors with surplus goods and families with genuine needs.

Vision

A compassionate society where zero usable goods are wasted and every community member receives timely help and essential resources with dignity and trust.
